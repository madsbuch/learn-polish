**Mieć** means *to have*:

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **mam** — I have | **mamy** — we have |
| 2nd | **masz** — you have | **macie** — you (all) have |
| 3rd | **ma** — he/she has | **mają** — they have |

- *Mam brata.* — I have a brother.
- *Masz czas?* — Do you have time?
- *Oni mają duży dom.* — They have a big house.

### Age
Polish *has* years: *Mam trzydzieści lat.* — I am thirty (literally "I have thirty years").
*Ile masz lat?* — How old are you?

### "Not having"
After *nie mam*, the thing you lack changes form (it goes into the genitive case):
- *Mam czas.* → *Nie mam czasu.* — I don't have time.
- *Mam samochód.* → *Nie mam samochodu.* — I don't have a car.

You don't need the rule yet — learn *nie mam czasu* as a phrase.
