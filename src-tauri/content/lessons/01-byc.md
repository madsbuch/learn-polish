**Być** means *to be*. It is irregular, so learn it as a block:

| | singular | | plural | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | ja **jestem** | I am | my **jesteśmy** | we are |
| 2nd | ty **jesteś** | you are | wy **jesteście** | you (all) are |
| 3rd | on/ona/ono **jest** | he/she/it is | oni/one **są** | they are |

Polish usually **drops the pronoun**, because the ending already says who it is:
*Jestem w domu.* — I am at home.

### Saying who or what
- *To jest Anna.* — This is Anna.
- *To jest mój dom.* — This is my house.
- *Jestem Mads.* — I am Mads.

### Describing
Adjectives after *być* agree with the person:
- *Jestem zmęczony.* — I am tired (a man speaking).
- *Jestem zmęczona.* — I am tired (a woman speaking).
- *Oni są zajęci.* — They are busy.

### Questions and "no"
- *Czy jesteś w domu?* — Are you at home? (*czy* turns a sentence into a yes/no question)
- *Nie jestem głodny.* — I am not hungry. (*nie* goes right before the verb)
